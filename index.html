ABOUT THE TECHFEST

THE GENESIS OF ALODANAX

ALODANAX is the flagship annual technical symposium organized by the Department of Information Technology & Computer Science. The symposium serves as a dynamic platform for innovation, technical excellence, and creative problem-solving, bringing together student developers, algorithmic thinkers, cybersecurity enthusiasts, and competitive gamers from across Mumbai. Through a diverse range of technical, non-technical, and gaming competitions, ALODANAX fosters collaboration, encourages emerging talent, and celebrates the spirit of technological innovation.
