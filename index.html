ABOUT THE TECHFEST

THE GENESIS OF ALODANAX

ALODANAX is the premier annual technical symposium orchestrated by the Department of Information Technology & Computer Science. Built at the intersection of precision engineering and raw creativity, the symposium convenes student developers, algorithmic designers, cybersecurity operatives, and competitive gamers from across the nation.

VISION

To cultivate an elite competitive forum that bridges foundational computer science with production-grade engineering, accelerating student innovation across artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and distributed systems.

PURPOSE

Empowering the next generation of technologists through rigorous hackathons, competitive problem-solving arenas, creative digital showcases, and direct industry collaboration.
